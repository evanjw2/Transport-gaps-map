Coverage, privacy & Google Maps

This is our own geographic explorer, not an overlay inside Google's website. Transit markers are approximate, hand-placed station centres for fictional examples—not exact platforms, entrances, track geometry or operator-confirmed incidents. Notices without a trustworthy location stay in the list, not on the map.

This is one saved municipal source, not all Munich disruptions or a live feed. Construction coverage is strongest in central districts and on main roads. No-parking notices are not whole-road closures. Multiple footprints may describe the same works.

Strikes, demonstrations, traffic jams, vehicle faults and lift outages are not comprehensively covered. Source schedules do not independently confirm current conditions. Unknown impacts stay unknown.

Street tiles are fetched from the Bavarian mapping service when enabled; it receives your IP address and viewed tile area. No location tracking, analytics or Google history access.

For anchored polygons on a Google map, export KML below, unzip it and import each file as a layer in Google My Maps. This is a manual snapshot import, not an automatic layer in the normal Google Maps page.
