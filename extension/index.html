How this works

Every morning a small script checks MVG service notices, official weather warnings, live weather for 14 districts, city roadworks, a few local news headlines and our team’s list of strikes, protests and big events. It stacks them up and tells you where your journey might be slowed down.

What the labels mean

  • LiveFrom the operator’s current notices, as of the last update.
  • OfficialPublished plans: MVG timetable changes, DWD warnings, city roadworks.
  • AnnouncedDated event with a public source (matches, markets, announced strikes).
  • News leadA headline that might matter. Not confirmed, so open the article.
  • ExampleMade-up scenario showing how protests, strikes or lift faults would appear. Not real.

Reading the map

  • 🟢 +3′ chipsLive average delay at 17 big stations (next 40 min). Tap one for its departure board.
  • ✕ red stopsStops MVG says are not served right now. ⇥ orange = where a line turns back.
  • ✓ green stopsNearby stops with other lines, a way around it. The dotted line is a straight-line walk, not a route.
  • Coloured lineTap a line in “Lines right now” (or a line badge under “Ways around it”) to draw its route through its stations, from MVV's timetable. Ends are labelled; tap any station for live departures.
  • Dashed ringsBusy areas where several things stack up. Not closures.

Privacy

No location tracking, accounts or analytics. Weather is refreshed from Open-Meteo in your browser; map tiles come from the Bavarian mapping service.
